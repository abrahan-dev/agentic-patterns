2026-07-28 · lunch Grilled chicken Caesar wraps

Ingredients

  • 1 medium (about 6 oz / 170 g) · boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 1 tablespoon · olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon · garlic powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon · salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon · black pepper
  • 2 cups, chopped · romaine lettuce
  • 2 tablespoons, grated · Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tablespoons · Caesar dressing
  • 2 medium · flour tortillas
  • 1 medium, cut into sticks · carrots

Method

  1. Preheat a grill pan or skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Pat the chicken dry. Rub with olive oil, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  3. Cook the chicken for 5 to 7 minutes per side, or until the center reaches 165°F (74°C). Let rest for 5 minutes, then slice thinly.
  4. In a bowl, toss romaine with Caesar dressing and Parmesan.
  5. Warm the tortillas briefly in a dry skillet or microwave so they are pliable.
  6. Divide the chicken and dressed lettuce between the tortillas, roll tightly, and slice if desired.
  7. Serve with carrot sticks on the side.
2026-07-28 · dinner Lemon herb salmon with rice and green beans

Ingredients

  • 1 fillet (about 6 oz / 170 g) · salmon fillet
  • 1 tablespoon · olive oil
  • 1/2, juiced and zested · lemon
  • 1 teaspoon dried or 1 tablespoon chopped fresh · dried dill or fresh dill
  • 1/4 teaspoon · garlic powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon · salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon · black pepper
  • 1/2 cup uncooked · white rice
  • 1 cup · water
  • 1 cup, trimmed · green beans
  • 1 teaspoon · butter or olive oil
  • to taste · salt and pepper

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a small baking tray with foil or parchment.
  2. Place the salmon on the tray. Drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice, then season with lemon zest, dill, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  3. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, until the salmon flakes easily with a fork.
  4. Meanwhile, rinse the rice. Combine rice and water in a small saucepan, bring to a boil, then cover and reduce to low heat. Cook for about 15 minutes, or until tender. Rest 5 minutes and fluff with a fork.
  5. Steam the green beans until bright green and tender-crisp, about 4 to 5 minutes. Toss with butter or olive oil and a little salt and pepper.
  6. Serve the salmon over the rice with green beans on the side.

2026-07-29 · dinner Beef stir-fry with broccoli and jasmine rice

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup uncooked · jasmine rice
  • 1 cup · water
  • 6 oz / 170 g, thinly sliced · beef sirloin or flank steak
  • 1 1/2 cups · broccoli florets
  • 1/2 medium, sliced · bell pepper
  • 2 cloves, minced · garlic
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons · soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon · oyster sauce or hoisin sauce
  • 1 teaspoon · cornstarch
  • 2 tablespoons · water
  • 1 tablespoon · vegetable oil
  • to taste · salt and pepper

Method

  1. Cook the jasmine rice according to package directions. Set aside covered.
  2. In a small bowl, mix soy sauce, oyster sauce, cornstarch, and 2 tablespoons water. Stir until smooth.
  3. Heat a skillet or wok over high heat. Add the oil.
  4. Add the beef in a single layer and sear for 1 to 2 minutes per side until browned. Remove to a plate.
  5. Add broccoli and bell pepper to the pan with a splash of water. Stir-fry for 3 to 4 minutes until crisp-tender.
  6. Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds, then return the beef to the pan.
  7. Pour in the sauce and toss everything until glossy and thickened, about 1 minute.
  8. Serve immediately over jasmine rice.
2026-07-30 · lunch Caprese pasta salad with grilled chicken

Ingredients

  • 2 oz / 55 g dry · short pasta such as rotini or penne
  • 1 medium (about 5 oz / 140 g) · boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 1 tablespoon · olive oil
  • 1/4 teaspoon · salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon · black pepper
  • 1/2 cup, halved · cherry tomatoes
  • 1/3 cup · fresh mozzarella pearls or diced mozzarella
  • 2 tablespoons, sliced · fresh basil
  • 1 tablespoon · balsamic vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon · olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon · honey or maple syrup
  • to taste · salt and pepper

Method

  1. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta until just tender. Drain and rinse under cool water to stop cooking.
  2. Season the chicken with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Grill in a grill pan or skillet over medium heat for 5 to 7 minutes per side, until cooked through. Rest, then slice.
  3. In a bowl, whisk balsamic vinegar, olive oil, honey, salt, and pepper.
  4. Combine the cooled pasta, tomatoes, mozzarella, basil, and sliced chicken.
  5. Toss with the dressing and chill for 10 minutes if desired before serving.

Method

2026-07-31 · dinner Pork tenderloin with roasted potatoes and asparagus

Ingredients

  • 1 small piece (about 6 oz / 170 g) · pork tenderloin
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons · olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon · garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon · dried thyme or rosemary
  • 1/2 teaspoon · salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon · black pepper
  • 1 medium, cut into 1-inch pieces · potatoes
  • 1 cup, trimmed · asparagus
  • 1 wedge · optional lemon wedge

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment or foil.
  2. Toss the potatoes with 1 tablespoon olive oil, half the salt, and a little pepper. Spread on the baking sheet and roast for 10 minutes.
  3. Rub the pork tenderloin with the remaining olive oil, garlic powder, thyme, remaining salt, and pepper.
  4. Push the potatoes to one side of the pan and add the pork. Roast for 15 to 20 minutes, or until the pork reaches 145°F (63°C) in the center.
  5. Add the asparagus to the pan for the last 8 to 10 minutes of cooking, tossing lightly with pan juices.
  6. Rest the pork for 5 minutes before slicing. Serve with potatoes and asparagus, plus lemon if desired.

Method

  7. Finish with parsley and serve immediately.
2026-08-02 · lunch Mushroom and spinach quiche with side salad

Ingredients

  • 1 single 9-inch crust · pie crust
  • 1 cup, sliced · mushrooms
  • 1 cup, chopped · spinach
  • 1 teaspoon · olive oil or butter
  • 2 large · eggs
  • 1/2 cup · milk or half-and-half
  • 1/4 teaspoon · salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon · black pepper
  • 1 pinch · nutmeg
  • 1/4 cup · shredded cheese
  • 2 cups · mixed greens
  • 1 tablespoon · vinaigrette

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Place the pie crust in a 9-inch pie plate and crimp the edges.
  2. Heat oil or butter in a skillet over medium heat. Cook the mushrooms for 4 to 5 minutes until browned, then add the spinach and cook until wilted. Let cool slightly.
  3. In a bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, salt, pepper, and nutmeg.
  4. Scatter the mushroom-spinach mixture and cheese into the crust. Pour the egg mixture over the top.
  5. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until the center is set and the top is lightly golden. Let rest 10 minutes before slicing.
  6. Toss the mixed greens with vinaigrette and serve alongside the quiche.
2026-08-02 · dinner Homemade chicken curry with basmati rice

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup uncooked · basmati rice
  • 1 cup · water
  • 6 oz / 170 g, bite-sized pieces · boneless skinless chicken thighs or breast
  • 1/4 medium, finely chopped · onion
  • 2 cloves, minced · garlic
  • 1 teaspoon, grated · ginger
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons · curry powder
  • 1 tablespoon · olive oil
  • 1/2 cup · coconut milk
  • 1/4 cup · chicken broth or water
  • 1/3 cup · peas
  • 1/4 teaspoon · salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon · black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon, chopped · cilantro, optional

Method

  1. Cook the basmati rice according to package directions and keep warm.
  2. Heat olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add onion and cook for 2 to 3 minutes until softened.
  3. Add garlic, ginger, and curry powder. Stir for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Add the chicken pieces and cook, stirring, until lightly browned on all sides.
  5. Pour in the coconut milk and broth or water. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 10 to 12 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through.
  6. Stir in the peas and cook for 2 minutes more. Season with salt and pepper.
  7. Serve the curry over basmati rice and garnish with cilantro if desired.

2026-08-03 · dinner Vegetable lasagna with garlic bread

Ingredients

  • 4 noodles · lasagna noodles
  • 1/2 cup · ricotta cheese
  • 1 · egg
  • 1 cup, chopped · spinach
  • 1/2 medium, diced · zucchini
  • 1 cup · tomato sauce
  • 1/2 cup, shredded · mozzarella cheese
  • 2 tablespoons, grated · Parmesan cheese
  • 1 teaspoon · olive oil
  • 1 small serving (1 to 2 slices) · garlic bread
  • to taste · salt and pepper

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Grease a small baking dish.
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package directions. Drain and lay flat.
  3. In a skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat. Cook the zucchini for 3 to 4 minutes, then add spinach and cook until wilted. Season lightly with salt and pepper.
  4. Mix ricotta with the egg and a pinch of salt and pepper.
  5. Spread a thin layer of tomato sauce in the bottom of the baking dish. Add a layer of noodles, ricotta mixture, vegetables, sauce, and mozzarella. Repeat until ingredients are used, finishing with sauce, mozzarella, and Parmesan on top.
  6. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes until bubbly and browned on top. Let rest 10 minutes before serving.
  7. Warm the garlic bread according to package directions or toast bread with butter and garlic. Serve alongside the lasagna.
